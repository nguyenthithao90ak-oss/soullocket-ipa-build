2. Eligibility and use

  • Provide truthful information. Do not impersonate others or use their accounts without permission.
  • Protect your account and promptly report suspected unauthorized access. Your responsibility depends on your conduct, fault and applicable law; you are not automatically responsible for everything an account hijacker does.

SoulLocket is not an emergency, treatment or professional advice service, or a guaranteed safety-monitoring tool.

3. Accounts, Houses and pairing

An account is used to sign in; a House is a space for personal or shared data. Pairing requires a valid invitation code/QR and the creator's approval. Pairing invitations, login QR codes and profile QR codes are different types of codes.

  • Invite only someone with whom you genuinely want to share a space; do not publish codes or scan login QR codes at a stranger's request.
  • The person entering the code moves to the creator's House. Both parties' existing data is not automatically merged; the entrant's old House may be cleaned up if no members remain. Back up before confirming.
  • Your House partner may view or manage shared content according to feature permissions. Do not use pairing to pressure someone into sharing location, photos or private information.
  • “Paired” is based on server membership status; choosing Couple mode or changing an avatar does not pair two accounts.

Sign-in or sensitive actions may require additional verification. See the invitation and QR guide.
