1. Accounts and shared spaces

Your account is used to sign in and manage access. A House is the space holding profiles, diaries, memories and shared content. You can use the app on your own; to connect, open Settings → Connect with your partner to create, enter or scan an invitation.

Once paired, the screen shows both avatars, names and the connection date; the shortcut outside this screen shows both avatars and the paired status. Choosing Couple mode does not replace pairing two accounts.

2. Feature groups

  • Home: avatars, day counters, presence and shortcuts.
  • Diaries/memories: personal reflections, saved photos and synchronized content.
  • Chat/community: content, interactions and sharing within each feature's scope.
  • Utilities, games and creative content available in your app version.
  • Settings, security, support and privacy choices.

Availability depends on the platform, version, device permissions and configuration. A feature file in the source code does not mean that feature is enabled or works in every release.

3. Data and infrastructure

SoulLocket uses Firebase for authentication, databases and notifications, together with Cloudflare R2/Workers for relevant file and service flows. Besides server data, devices may hold drafts, caches and downloads.

Your House partner may access shared content according to the feature; a PIN lock does not itself make shared content visible only to you. Before pairing, read the old House warning and back up anything you need to keep.

See the Privacy Policy for providers, data and your rights.

4. Limitations to know

SoulLocket does not guarantee absolute security, uninterrupted operation or recovery of every piece of data. It does not replace emergency services or professional advice. AI content, maps and presence status may be inaccurate or delayed.

Do not use SoulLocket to secretly track people, coerce others or share data you are not entitled to use.
